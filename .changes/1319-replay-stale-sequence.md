### Fixed

- **`@reticlehq/server` — a saved `act_sequence` step failed with the browser's raw `ref ... no longer resolves to an element` when the page re-rendered part way through it.** The step resolved every sub-step's anchor before anything ran and sent one batch, so an action that re-rendered the page killed the refs for the sub-steps after it, even though the same anchors were still on the page under new refs. Replay now takes the stale ref the page names, works out which sub-steps already ran, looks up only the rest again, and sends them, so nothing acts twice; a sequence walked sub-step by step does the same for each one. A target that is stale again at the same sub-step is reported with how far the sequence got (`sub-step 2 of 3; 2 before it ran`), and the replay is `unverifiable` instead of `error`: the steps before it stay in the report, the step that could not be acted on is among them with its index, and nothing says the app failed. Replay does not guess when it cannot be sure: sub-steps that share one element cannot be told apart by the ref the page names, so that failure is reported without a retry, and an anchor that the re-render made match several elements is reported as an `anchor_ambiguous` drift before anything is dispatched at it. Closes [#1319](https://github.com/reticlehq/reticle/issues/1319).
