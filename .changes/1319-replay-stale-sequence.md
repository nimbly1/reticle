### Fixed

- **`@reticlehq/server` — a saved `act_sequence` step failed with the browser's raw `ref ... no longer resolves to an element` when the page re-rendered part way through it.** The step resolved every sub-step's anchor before anything ran and sent one batch, so an action that re-rendered the page killed the refs for the sub-steps after it, even though the same anchors were still on the page under new refs. Replay now takes the stale ref the page names, works out which sub-steps already ran, looks up only the rest again, and sends them, so nothing acts twice; a sequence walked sub-step by step does the same for each one. A target that is stale again at the same sub-step is reported with how far the sequence got (`sub-step 2 of 3; 2 before it ran`), and the replay is `unverifiable` instead of `error`: the steps before it stay in the report, the step that could not be acted on is among them with its index, and nothing says the app failed. Closes [#1319](https://github.com/reticlehq/reticle/issues/1319).
